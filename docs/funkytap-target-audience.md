# Target Audience

The target audience of FunkyTap:

1. People with ADHD who regularly go to the gym
2. People who want to track their workout progress without cognitive overhead

## Hypothetical Portrait of the Primary User

### The ADHD Gym-Goer

1. Characteristics:
    - Age: 18–35
    - Gender: any
    - Goes to the gym 2–4 times per week
    - Diagnosed or self-identified ADHD
    - Uses one or multiple gym clubs
2. Technological literacy:
    - Confident smartphone user
    - Has tried and abandoned several fitness apps
    - Values simplicity over features

### The Focused Gym Regular

1. Characteristics:
    - Age: 18–40
    - Gender: any
    - Goes to the gym 2–5 times per week
    - No ADHD diagnosis, but prefers minimalist tools
    - Uses one or multiple gym clubs
2. Technological literacy:
    - Confident smartphone user
    - Has tried standard fitness trackers — too overwhelming
    - Wants a quick solution without complex setup or extra screens
